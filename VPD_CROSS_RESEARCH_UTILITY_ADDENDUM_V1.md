## Catalogs, selectors, and equal budgets

A catalog is a finite set of actions or artifacts. A selector is a rule that chooses one of them. The original utility audit's `q_monolithic`, `q_geometric`, and `q_interval_aware` names described rules; this addendum makes that distinction explicit. Regret is over the full feasible action/artifact catalog, not just the three selected winners. Recipe IDs are frozen now. A recipe with a missing component ID, module inventory, trained artifact, or hash is an unresolved slot, not a materialized candidate. No empirical run is ready until all such bindings are sealed without looking at final outcomes.

For each track the global (monolithic) selector maximizes calibration mean task utility. The geometric selector maximizes a frozen discovery geometry score. The interval selector minimizes worst-case calibration regret subject to a benign-loss upper bound of 0.01; ties prefer larger utility lower bounds, then baseline and catalog order. If no candidate meets the screen, return the unchanged incumbent/ABSTAIN and mark the claim unsupported. The same catalog, calibration observations, rank/fit caps, seed blocks, and deadline apply to all three. Geometry may ignore calibration outcomes, but its reserved observation allowance is not donated to additional fitting or candidates. Report both actual cost and the common maximum allowance.

No new VPD or SAE fits occur within these selector comparisons. Historical fit cost is a sunk cost that must still be disclosed; shared read/cache cost is counted once for the campaign and charged to each selector in a standalone deployment comparison. Report a Pareto table of utility versus physical resources. The primary score is raw task utility with resource constraints, avoiding the original audit's unspecified conversion of FLOPs or bytes into utility. No fitted cost penalty may be introduced after reveal.

The concrete numerical ceilings in the manifest bound the proposed screens. They are design choices, not historical measured usage or hardware forecasts. A full binding must show the existing recipe fits these ceilings; if it does not, version the proposal before any run. Unused baseline/ABSTAIN budgets are not redistributed. Failed fits, retries, rolled-back updates, counterfactual evaluations, and discovery costs all consume the applicable envelope. The current document and statistics work has zero model calls and zero model fitting, but its CPU work and newly written files plainly have nonzero cost; unknown historical FLOPs/storage must be null rather than zero.

Physical accounting keeps five quantities separate: model requests and actual forward/backward invocations; fit/capture/selector/evaluation FLOPs; persistent/read bytes and peak memory; coordinates and update norms; benign-utility loss. A rank-one dense VPD matrix changes many weight entries. Nanbeige's repeated invocation does not double physical matrix rank, but it does increase intervention applications and compute. A one-dimensional SAE steering direction may write all 3,072 hidden coordinates at every selected token/pass. Count feature coordinates, physical scalar entries, and repeated write events separately.

## Per-track application and boundaries

All active actions have activation subspace dimension one. The frozen dictionary, prompt handling, quantization, KV precision, greedy sampler, and 8,192-token cap remain matched. A direction and its scalar dose are fixed before evaluation.

Discovery geometry is distance to a correct-minus-incorrect task-state direction. It is unavailable if discovery lacks either class; final labels cannot repair that failure. The label-only selector receives pass IDs and a discovery-only mapping to actions, never final answers or coordinate effects. Pair all conditions within complete ARC tasks, logic problems, or their shared generator families. Multiple ARC test inputs are not independent trials. Put near-duplicate templates and all retries in the same block.

The existing answer-steering archive already has zero complete-unit successes in all seven arms, with 62/70 outputs capped. It supplies a negative/exploratory audit, not fresh selection evidence. Random controls and new family-disjoint blocks are missing. The inspected feature/dose cannot be called prospectively selected on that same cohort. The two existing packs used to fit the shared pass axis are still not a third-domain test. The 0/36 separate-dictionary admissions stand.

Keep Q4 versus BF16, AdamW versus Muon, generation budgets, and loop counts as separate strata. An optimizer comparison requires matching training tokens/steps, total dictionary capacity and actual fit FLOPs; a dictionary's EV improvement is an auxiliary outcome until a downstream intervention improves task utility. Historical Hermes 27B scores cannot enter paired regret without the original prompts, matched runtime budgets, and grader contracts. The historical answer-selection oracle is diagnostic hindsight, not a reachable selector.

## Simultaneous uncertainty and a declared error set

For each independently sampled family block, aggregate complete-case task success and a separate benign score into [0,1]. All descendants of a common template/world remain in one block. Selection uses discovery/calibration only. Freeze candidate identities and all three selector outputs before opening final data. The helper takes the final block table; it never fits candidates, calls a model, or chooses a new final winner.

For K feasible candidates including baseline, construct simultaneous mean intervals using a union bound over M=2K task/benign coordinates. For n independent blocks per coordinate use `h = sqrt(log(2M/alpha)/(2n))`, clipped to [0,1], with alpha=0.05. Dependence among candidates within one family is allowed; independence between family blocks is required and cannot be inferred from distinct ID strings. Results are conditional on the frozen candidate fits/seeds. This is a conservative finite-sample alternative to the original proposed small-sample studentized bootstrap. It makes no unverified exact-coverage claim for eight families. A four-track combined claim would require allocating alpha across tracks before analysis.

Declare `E_rho = {e: |e_j| <= rho for every catalog candidate}` for rho in {0, 0.02, 0.05}. Expand each mean interval by rho and intersect with [0,1]. This is sensitivity to specified score error, not an estimate of transfer uncertainty. The primary result uses rho=0; always report the two stress settings as well. Self-comparisons cancel exactly. With resulting `[L_j,U_j]` and baseline b:

* Improvement interval for q: `[L_q-U_b, U_q-L_b]` (exactly [0,0] if q=b).
* Regret envelope: `[max(0,max_(p!=q)(L_p-U_q)), max(0,max_(p!=q)(U_p-L_q))]`.
* Benign loss upper bound: baseline benign upper endpoint minus q's benign lower endpoint (exactly zero for unchanged baseline).

Report all possible challengers with `U_p > L_q`, bound-attaining challenger IDs, the mean-best observed candidate, incomplete cells, and outcome-independent infeasibility reasons. Completely unmeasured candidates retain [0,1]. Never silently shrink the reachable set to measured winners. Censored but validly graded failures may score zero per the pinned grader; missing, transport-failed or ungradable results are unknown. The conservative helper sets a candidate mean interval to [0,1] when any declared cell is missing, making incomplete comparisons visibly weak.

Thresholds for the bounded design are positive baseline lower bound, regret upper bound at most 0.02, and benign-loss upper bound at most 0.01, in addition to track gates. Statistical overlap means inconclusive evidence, not proof of equivalence or falsification. A control's strictly positive simultaneous advantage is a directional falsifier. Report geometric/global/interval selected actions against the same full catalog, not merely against baseline. A candidate may improve on baseline yet retain large regret.

The proposed 16-block caps are feasibility screens, not powered certification studies. These conservative intervals will normally be too wide to establish 0.01 benign preservation or 0.02 regret. A passing synthetic calculator test cannot resolve that limitation. If results are inconclusive, retain the incumbent and use only discovery/calibration variance or predeclared distribution-free precision to design a separately versioned larger study; never continue sampling the revealed final set until significance.
