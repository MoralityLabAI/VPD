#### V2 fresh-seed feature-replica matcher

The historical identity receipt uses a same-index diagnostic because both Q4 fits share the same seed and example IDs. That is appropriate for its narrow provenance claim, but it is not a sufficient matching rule for independently trained dictionaries. Absolute cosine can falsely accept a decoder sign reversal even though SAE codes are nonnegative, and unconstrained nearest neighbors can assign several source features to the same target.

The V2 matcher fixes those failure modes without rewriting history:

- signed decoder cosine, so an opposite decoder direction is rejected;
- mutual nearest-neighbor matching, so an asymmetric nearest match is rejected;
- one-to-one feature pairs, so duplicate collapse cannot inflate replication;
- optional training-only category profiles, so geometrically similar decoders with swapped category behavior are rejected;
- row-blocked similarity evaluation, so the full cosine matrix need not be retained.

Synthetic benchmark outcomes: 4/4 expected outcomes reproduced—clean noisy permutation recovered; sign flip rejected; duplicate collapse rejected; category-profile swap rejected. This validates the matcher implementation, not any new biological or model-mechanistic claim.

- Joint-factor capacity gate: current `pass × phase` routing beats the four-way random mean at 3/3 sites but loses to monolithic, pass-only, and phase-only at 3/3. Do not multiply dictionaries across category factors. Prefer a shared SAE with factor-labeled feature groups unless a fresh pack reverses all simpler-arm comparisons after every parent category admits.
- Feature-interpretation gate: a hidden-state category admission does not imply that a frozen SAE is valid in the same phase. Every phase/category/site used for feature claims must independently clear held-out reconstruction quality. The current prompt frozen-SAE archives fail at all three sites and may be used only as negative controls.
- Feature-identity gate: replicated feature counts are not replicated feature identities. Decoder alignment must use the sealed V2 signed-cosine, mutual-nearest-neighbor, one-to-one matcher and clear a preregistered direction threshold, minimum count and bidirectional coverage per category, label-permutation control, reconstruction-quality gate, and fresh-seed/family-disjoint replication. Current final-norm selective-stratum identity is provisional only; operative admissions remain zero. Absolute-cosine or collision-permitting matching is a hard stop, and selective-stratum replication must never be labeled full-dictionary replication.
- Seed-independence gate: an identity replication must change initialization seed. Changing the SAE loss objective while holding the seed fixed is a robustness control, not independent replication. The layer-21 control's 0/6 cross-seed supports versus 3/3 fixed-seed cross-objective supports makes this requirement empirical; it cannot compensate for reconstruction failure and does not establish stability at layer 11 or final norm.
- Category-subspace gate: individual-feature instability is not evidence of subspace instability. Test category spans directly, correct across every searched seed/site family, and require an independently seeded, reconstruction-qualified replication. The layer-21 rank-16 control has 0/6 familywise-corrected cross-seed supports versus 3/3 fixed-seed cross-objective supports. The 2K/8K same-seed comparison supports pass-category spans at 3/3 sites and is quality-qualified at layer 11 and final norm, but still authorizes no compartment because seed and example IDs are reused. Rank sensitivity is descriptive and cannot provide alternate admission attempts.
- Equal-budget arms: monolithic SAE, pass-compartmentalized SAEs, and a matched random partition.
- Exact trainable parameter budget per arm: 37,754,880. Activations are training-role centered and output bias is fixed at zero, preventing the two-dictionary arms from receiving an extra trainable output-bias vector.
- Training seeds: 1707, 250829, and 260829.
- Failed or tied comparison action: retain the monolithic dictionary.

1. one monolithic SAE;
2. one SAE per admitted coarse category, with no unsupported subclusters;
3. randomly partitioned SAEs matched for dictionary count, width, tokens, optimizer steps, and seeds.

Primary endpoints must be held-out reconstruction explained variance at matched L0, category selectivity, cross-category leakage, and stability across seeds. ## Paper-facing implication

The result sharpens the paper's safety argument. Category-first mechanistic analysis can identify a strong, reproducible coarse transition while correctly refusing unsupported fine structure. That is exactly the kind of brake needed before mechanistic signals are converted into an RL policy or recursive improvement loop: a visible separation is not permission to invent mechanisms, train specialized dictionaries, or optimize behavior.
